Ingestion Unstructured – Part 1

Workshop 5 — Processing of unstructured data

Part 1: AI Ingestion, Enrichment and Silver

Layer. Theme: Audio customer calls — Renewable Energy Sector

Business context

SolarVoix is a French renewable energy player (solar panels, heat pumps, VE charging stations). Their customer relationship center receives 120 appels par semaine. Today, these calls are saved in MP3, archived in a network folder, and No one analyses them . Result: Disgruntled customers leave without the company knowing, and the rate of churn rises.

Problem: How to automatically detect risk calls (very negative feeling, intention to terminate) to alert the VAS manager in real time?

Architecture cible (3 parties) :

Audio files (synthetic MP3)
▼
Lakehouse Bronze ← raw ingestion
▼
Notebook PySpark + Analysis IA (transformers, without API key)
▼
Lakehouse Silver ← transcripts + enriched scores
▼
Pipeline Data Factory + Activator (Part 2)
▼
Semantic model + Power BI Fabric Report (Part 3)

💡 Pedagogical choice: This workshop uses no paid Azure key. . IA transcription and analysis are based on open-source libraries (transformers, pipeline HuggingFace). You can replay the workshop indefinitely without external dependence.
