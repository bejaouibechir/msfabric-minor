Objective

At the end of this workshop, you will have created 6 optimized analytical tables ready for Power BI and real-time decision making:

  1. daily_consumption_by_site — Detailed daily consumption per site

- consumption_predictions — ML predictions and associated errors

Horizon temporel : 30 days of data (180 days × 6 hourly sites, daily/month aggregated)

Cell 15: Calculation of erasure potential (curtailing)

Before executing

For each eligible hour, we calculate:

  1. ratio_vs_baseline = actual consumption / baseline 7 j
    - > 1: overload → strong erasure opportunity
    - ~1 : charge normale → effacement moins pertinent

  2. curtailment_potential_mw = 30 % maximum reduction (industry standard)
    - A site cannot reduce by more than 30% without operational risk

  3. potential_gain_eur = financial economy if erased
    - Formula: (MW reduced) × (Prix spot - Prix effacement contraacue)
    - Prix_effacement = seuil contractuel (102 €/MWh IND_001, 116 €/MWh IND_002)

  4. action_signal = operational recommendation
    - Depends on the instant price

💻 Code

df_curtailment = df_flex.withColumn(
    "ratio_vs_baseline",
    F.round(F.col("avg_consumption_mw") / F.col("baseline_7d_mw"), 2)
).withColumn(
    "curtailment_potential_mw",
    F.round(F.col("avg_consumption_mw") * 0.3, 3)         # 30% effacement
).withColumn(
    "potential_gain_eur",
    F.when(
        F.col("price_eur_mwh") > F.col("curtailment_price_eur_mwh"),
        F.round((F.col("avg_consumption_mw") * 0.3) *
                (F.col("price_eur_mwh") - F.col("curtailment_price_eur_mwh")), 2)
    ).otherwise(0)
).withColumn(
    "action_signal",
    F.when(F.col("price_eur_mwh") > 300, "🔴 EFFACEMENT MAX")
     .when(F.col("price_eur_mwh") > 200, "🟠 EFFACEMENT PARTIEL")
     .when(F.col("price_eur_mwh") < 50,  "🟢 CONSOMMER")
     .otherwise("⚪ Surveiller")
)

Results

(Colones added to DataFrame, no direct display)

Interpretation

The new columns allow automated decision-making :
- 🔴 EFFACEMENT MAX : Energy crisis, reduce immediately → warns operators
- 🟠 EFFACEMENT PARTIEL : High price, optional but profitable erasure
- 🟢 CONSOMMER : Cheap electricity, maintain full charge
- ⚪ Surveiller : Situation stable, attendre nouvelles conditions

Encrypted example :
- SITE_IND_001, heure H : consommation 2,5 MW, prix spot 250 €/MWh, prix_contrat 102 €/MWh
- Potential reduction: 2.5 × 0.3 = 0.75 MW
- Gain potentiel : 0,75 × (250 - 102) = 111 € this hour only
- Signal : 🟠 EFFACEMENT PARTIEL

Cell 19: Qualification and prioritization of alerts

Before executing

Raw warnings are transformed into tickets actionnables :

  1. alert_type — Trade category (fast diagnostics)
    - "Anormal consumption" → Technical team (check site)
    - "Very high spot price" → Trading Team (opportunity erasure)
    - "High prediction error" → Data science (fine model)

  2. priority — Escalade
    - CRITICAL: Anomaly + price > 300 → immediate action
    - HIGH : Either anomaly or high price
    - MEDIUM: Isolated prediction error
    - BASSE : Autres cas

This approach ensures that the operator sees first situations requiring prompt decision.
