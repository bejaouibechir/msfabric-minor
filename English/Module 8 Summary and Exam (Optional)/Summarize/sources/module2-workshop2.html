Pipeline Workshop – Case Study 2

Workshop 2: Full Medallion Architecture — Bronze → Silver → Gold with orchestration

Context: Urban transition to an intelligent energy grid

Background

One ville intelligente Upgrades its power grid by deploying 100 IoT sensors to monitor consumption, detect anomalies and reduce energy losses.

1. Status

Raw data arrive continuously and must be cleaned, enriched with reference thresholds, and analyzed for incidents.

  • 100 IoT sensors deployed in 4 neighbourhoods
  • 7200 readings 3 days (real time data)
  • 50 incidents automatically detected
  • Gross data Unstructured cleaning
  • Reference table with warning thresholds by sensor type

2. Issue

How to transform raw IoT data (Bronze) into cleaned and enriched data (Silver), by detecting anomalies, applying business rules and closing with Until to deal with unresolved incidents?

Contraintes :

  • Lookup (from table)
  • Filter active sensors with If Condition
  • Until
  • Trace detected anomalies

3. Solution : Pipeline de transformation intelligent

Pipeline qui :

  1. Charge the 5 CSV to Bronze tables (without _ ni -)
  2. Lit the reference table with Lookup
  3. Copie and transforme Bronze → Silver
  4. Filtre valid data with If Condition
  5. Boucle with Until until incident resolution
  6. Trace Treatment

Files required

Download the 5 CSV files fournis :

  • infrastructures_existantes.csv (100 sensors)
  • monitoring_iot.csv (7200 readings)
  • incidents_alertes.csv (50 incidents)
  • metriques_performance.csv (28 metrics)
  • seuils_alertes_ref.csv (4 thresholds - Reference table)

Guided Analysis Questions

Question 1: Volume of IoT data

Watch the table monitoring :

SELECT COUNT(*) as TotalReleves FROM monitoring

Expected result 7200 readings

Interpretation :

  • 7200 3-day surveys = 2400 surveys/day
  • 2400 readings/day ÷ 100 sensors = 24 readings/sensor/day
  • Conclusion : Chaque capteur envoie 1 reading per hour (24h × 3 days = 72 surveys/sensor)

💡 Business impact : Measure the frequency of monitoring to detect anomalies in near real time.

Question 2: Geographical distribution

Watch the table infrastructures :

SELECT Quartier, COUNT(*) as NbCapteurs 
FROM infrastructures 
GROUP BY Quartier
ORDER BY NbCapteurs DESC

Result achieved :

Quartier Nb Sensors
Quartier_Residentiel 28
Centre_Ville 26
Zone_Commerciale 23
Zone Industrielle 23

Interpretation :

  • Distribution slightly unequal sensors (23-28 depending on the area)
  • The Residential is best covered (28 sensors), consistent with higher population density
  • The Industrial Zone and Commercial Zone are less covered (23 sensors each), which may represent a risk if consumption is more variable or higher

💡 Business impact : The coverage is generally satisfying, but particular attention should be paid to the Industrial Zone — less covered but potentially at higher risk of energy anomaly. It would be appropriate to add 2 to 5 additional sensors to balance monitoring.

Question 3: Sensor condition

Watch the table infrastructures :

SELECT Statut, COUNT(*) as NbCapteurs
FROM infrastructures
GROUP BY Statut

Expected result :

Statut Nb Sensors
Actif 55
En_Maintenance 27

Interpretation :

  • 55% sensors are operational (55/100)
  • 27% are in maintenance (En Maintenance)
  • 5% are down (En Panne)
  • 13% have uninformed status (55+27+5 = 87 out of 100)

💡 Business impact Availability rate below recommended threshold (< 70%). There is an urgent need to plan the commissioning of En Maintenance sensors and to invest En Panne sensors to at least 70% availability.

Question 4: Criticality of incidents

⚠️ Alerte : Critique + High 48% of incidents (24/50) — almost half. The criticality pyramid is reversed Too many serious incidents compared to weak incidents.

💡 Business impact : This incident profile reveals a live network. It is imperative to deal urgently with the 24 critical/high incidents before they impact the city's electrical distribution. Enhanced preventive maintenance is recommended.

KPI 1: Network coverage rate

SELECT
    COUNT(*)                                                         AS TotalCapteurs,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Statut = 'Actif' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)               AS CapteursActifs,
    ROUND(
        100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN Statut = 'Actif' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
              / COUNT(*), 1
