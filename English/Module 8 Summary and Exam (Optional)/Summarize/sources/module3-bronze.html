The objective of the workshop

The Bronze layer of a Medallion Architecture represents the foundation of your data infrastructure. It receives raw data directly from source systems, without any transformation, cleaning or business logic. The aim of this first workshop is to load five CSV files from a fleet of six power production and consumption sites in France, converting them into persistent Delta tables within a Microsoft Fabric lakehouse.

Why this approach? The Bronze layer acts like a source of unchangeable truth. It preserves the data exactly as it comes from IoT sensors, SCADA systems and operational databases, including with their defects (null values, error codes, heteroclite formats). This traceability is essential for audit, debugging and regulatory compliance. It is only in Silver layer that you will apply business transformations and cleaning.

At the end of this workshop, you will have created five Bronze tables, performed an initial quality diagnosis, and identified the anomalies that will be treated downstream. You will also have a solid basis to understand the Medallion architecture and its progress raw data → cleaned data → business data.

Business context

Your organisation operates a network of six electricity production and consumption sites in France. Two industrial sites (capacity 5 MW and 3.5 MW), two commercial sites (2 MW and 1.5 MW) and two residential sites (0.8 MW and 0.6 MW). Each site is equipped with IoT sensors that transmit electricity consumption every 15 minutes for 30 days. Additional data come from several sources: EPEX market spot prices, weather conditions, site reference data and maintenance events.

The problem: these raw data arrive in various formats (incoherent timestamps, missing or invalid numerical values represented by error codes like -888 or -999), with duplicates and uneven quality depending on the source. Before using this data for analysis or machine learning, it is necessary first to centralize it, preserve its integrity, and document anomalies. That's exactly the role of the Bronze Layer.

Cell 4: Consumption raw quality diagnostic

Before executing

You now perform a statistical diagnosis of the table consumption_raw. This aggregate SQL query has several quality dimensions: the total number of rows, the number of unique combinations (timestamp, site id) to detect duplicates, null values (total absence), negative values (error codes), and outliers (consumption > 10 MW, abnormally high). These metrics form the quality line base for the Bronze layer and will serve as a benchmark to assess the impact of Silver cleaning.

💻 Code

%%sql
-- Count NULL, error codes, duplicates
SELECT
CUNT(*) as total rows,
CUNT(DISTINCT timestamp, site id) as unique rows,
CUNT(*) - CUNT(DISTINCT timestamp, site id) as duplicates,
SUM(CASE WHEN consumption mw IS NULL THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as null values,
SUM(CASE WHEN consumption mw < 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as error codes,
SUM(CASE WHEN consumption mw > 10 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as external
From bronze.consumption raw

Results

total_rows unique_rows duplicates null_values error_codes outliers
18144 17280 864 554 319 27

Interpretation

This diagnosis quantifies anomalies and guides cleaning priorities. Total of 18,144 lines, but only 17,280 unique combinations (timestamp, site id), revealing 864 doublons (4.8 per cent of data). Typically, these duplicates come from retransmissions following network timeouts or sensor recalibration. They must be deduplicated in Silver, keeping the most recent or reliable line depending on the business context.

554 valeurs NULL (3%) for consumption_mw are sensor failures or undocumented maintenance periods. In Silver, you will choose to filter or charge them (linear interpolation, average site value).

319 error codes (1.8%): negative values codifying failure states (-888 = sensor defect, -999 = network failure, -777 = calibration in progress). These codes must be identified, mapped to business categories, then filtered or treated specifically according to the application logic.

27 outliers (0.15%) with consumption > 10 MW exceed the theoretical maximum capacity of any site (5 MW). These anomalies justify an investigation: error of magnitude, poorly applied multiplier, or exceptional business event (external electricity import). To be examined in detail during the Silver.

These figures provide a solid diagnosis: approximately 6-7 per cent of the data show identified anomalies. This is a very reasonable rate for raw IoT data in production.

Summary of the Bronze Workshop

Objectives achieved

You successfully conducted the complete ingestion of raw data for your electricity generation site fleet. Five CSV files (18.144 + 2.880 + 720 + 6 + 8 = 21.758 lines) were loaded in Delta format within the lakehouse Lakehouse_bronze, in a dedicated scheme bronze. No transformations were applied: all anomalies (heterogeneous timestamp formats, null values, error codes, duplicates, outliers) were preserved, documented and quantified.

Technical key points

Architecture Delta Lake : You used the Delta format, the Parquet evolution for the data lakehouse. Delta offers ACID guarantees (Atomicity, Coherence, Isolation, Durability), versioning, and performance optimization. SaveAsTable() data persists in Fabric managed table.

Idempotence : Orders CREATE SCHEMA IF NOT EXISTS and mode overwrite ensure that re-executing the notebook does not create errors or duplicates. This is essential in production.

Spark API : You have combined the PySpark API (reading CSV, count, write) and SQL (SELECT, CUNT DISTINCT, CASE WHEN aggregations). Both models coexist in Fabric; choose according to readability and performance.

Business Key Points

Six operational sites : Heterogeneous park (2 industrial 5-3.5 MW, 2 commercial 2-1.5 MW, 2 residential 0.8-0.6 MW) captured with granularity 15 min over 30 days.

Dimensions enrichies : Consumption data enriched by spot prices (EPEX, 2,880 measurements), weather (720 hourly measurements), site references (6 masters), maintenance (8 events). These dimensions will allow multi-criteria analysis (consumption-temperature correlation, maintenance impact, price optimization).

10,3 % d'anomalies : Acceptable but targeted rate. Duplicates (4.8%), NULL (3%), error codes (1.8%) and external (0.15) require differential treatment according to application logic (filtering vs. imputation vs. investigation).

Key points for Silver

The Silver layer will apply business transformations based on this Bronze diagnosis. The priorities framed by your report (timestamps normalization, deduplication, error code mapping, null management, external validation, enrichment) will guide the Silver Notebook and avoid ineffective adhoc analysis.

Bronze layer remains immutable : you will always keep the original intact, allowing full audit and replay of treatments if a Silver logic proves to be wrong.
